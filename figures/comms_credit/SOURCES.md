## What the figure is about

All three panels post-train **the same** supervised checkpoint,
`run_paper_split_v2/checkpoint-2500`. That checkpoint is
`posttrain_common.DEFAULT_INIT_CHECKPOINT`
(`/home/wjl86/anticipation3/posttrain_common.py`, line 44) and is passed explicitly as
`--init_checkpoint run_paper_split_v2/checkpoint-2500` by all three job scripts
(`train_grpo.sbatch:67`, `train_ppo_production.sbatch:55`, `train_ppo_f1.sbatch:46`).
The model reconstructs a symbolic *score* from an expressive piano *performance*
(ASAP corpus, paper split).

**Protocol** (docstring of `/home/wjl86/anticipation3/visualizer/rl_f1_table.py`):
"unfiltered (`raw`) performance input, no ground-truth seeding, greedy AR rollout,
macro-averaged over pieces so every piece carries equal weight."

**Criterion definitions** (docstring of `/home/wjl86/anticipation3/visualizer/compute_f1.py`):

- `onset_pitch` — pitch AND onset must match exactly on the window's bin grid
- `onset_pitch_dur` — pitch AND onset AND duration must all match exactly
- `onset_pitch_tol1` — pitch must match, onset may differ by up to ±1 bin

Matching is one-to-one, so duplicated predictions cannot inflate the score. One bin is
10 ms (`TIME_RESOLUTION = 100` bins/second, `anticipation/config.py`; see CLAUDE.md).

**Which 24 windows.** The header of `/home/wjl86/anticipation3/visualizer/data.js` gives
`example_order = ["val-01" … "val-12", "test-01" … "test-12"]`, `seed: 17`,
`val_file: "data/val_paper.txt"`, `test_file: "data/test_paper.txt"` — 12 validation
pieces and 12 test pieces, one window each, 24 distinct pieces.

## Limitations that the caption states, and their evidence

1. **No run reached its step budget — all three were killed by the SLURM scheduler.**
   - GRPO: `/home/wjl86/anticipation3/logs/onpolicy_grpo_982083.out` ends
     `step 2424/5750 …` followed by
     `*** JOB 982083 ON thickstun-compute-01 CANCELLED AT 2026-08-14T00:40:38 DUE to SIGNAL Terminated ***`
   - PPO: `/home/wjl86/anticipation3/logs/ppo_corrected_28071.out` ends
     `step 4975/6000 …` then `*** JOB 28071 … CANCELLED AT 2026-08-14T10:54:53 …`
   - PPO-F1: `/home/wjl86/anticipation3/logs/ppo_f1_49620.out` ends `step 5310 …` then
     `*** JOB 49620 … CANCELLED AT 2026-08-14T22:10:10 …`; `MAX_STEPS` defaults to 6000
     in `train_ppo_f1.sbatch:31`.

2. **Single seed per arm.** No repeats of any arm exist in the repository.

3. **No dispersion (error bars) on panel (b), and none can be recovered.**
   `visualizer/rl_f1_table.py` reads per-window F1 out of `visualizer/data.js`, but that
   file was rewritten on 2026-08-22 by the N-best rerank pipeline and its current
   contents no longer include the `rollouts_valloss`, `rollouts_grpo` or `rollouts_ppo`
   blocks (verified by loading it: the surviving rollout groups are `rollouts`,
   `rollouts_lora`, `rollouts_masked*`, `rollouts_mbr`, `rollouts_paper1/2`, and the
   `rollouts_rerank*` family). Only the aggregated 2026-08-14 outputs survive. Rather
   than invent error bars, the figure plots the aggregates and the caption states n.

4. **Panel (b)'s 24 pieces are not all fully held out.** Twelve come from the validation
   split, which was used to pick checkpoints; twelve come from the test split, which was
   not. Both are held out of gradient updates.

5. **A real noise reference, from `reward_vs_f1_diagnosis.json`.** On the `filtered`
   stream, splitting the same 24 pieces into its 12 validation and 12 test halves moves
   the ranking around: PPO's onset+pitch F1 is 0.173913 on the validation half and
   0.211353 on the test half, while the supervised checkpoint is 0.224034 and 0.203502.
   A one- to two-point gap at n = 24 is therefore not resolvable. (Quoted in the caption.)

6. **Panels are not cross-comparable in absolute terms.** Panels (a) and (b) concern arms
   trained on `data/train_paper.txt` and evaluated on the paper split; panel (c)'s arm
   trains on `data/train_paper_unfiltered.txt` and validates on
   `data/val_paper_unfiltered.txt` — a different, mistakes-included repacking of the same
   corpus (see CLAUDE.md, "Data variants"). Only the direction of change within a panel
   should be read.

7. **Panel (a) and panel (c) both have a series called REWARD, but they are different
   quantities**: [0, 3] accuracy-sum in (a), [0, 1] F1 in (c). This is called out
   explicitly in CLAUDE.md and is why the two are on separate axes with separate units.
